# ProtonX Power LLC

**Field-Activated Power Systems for Mission-Critical and Off-Grid Applications**

ProtonX Power LLC is an early-stage U.S. technology company developing field-activated, high-energy-density power systems for demanding defense, commercial, and remote-operation environments.

Our approach is designed around a simple change in how energy is carried and supplied: carry a reusable power-generation module and inactive consumable fuel, then activate and generate electricity when power is needed.

---

### Core Concept

- **Power On Demand:** Generate electricity when required rather than carrying all energy as pre-stored battery capacity.
- **Reduced Carried System Weight:** Targeting substantially lower carried system weight for extended-duration power requirements compared with comparable conventional battery-based configurations.*
- **Field Activation:** Designed for activation at the point of use using locally available aqueous fluids, where appropriate.
- **Reduced Charging Dependence:** Designed to reduce dependence on conventional charging infrastructure and pre-positioned power resources.
- **Reusable Generation Module:** The power-generation module is intended to be carried and reused while inactive consumable fuel supports additional operating duration.
