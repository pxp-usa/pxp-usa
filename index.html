ABOUT PROTONX POWER

A deep-tech company developing a different approach to mission power

ProtonX Power LLC is an early-stage U.S. technology company developing field-activated, high-energy-density power systems for demanding applications.

The company is developing a different approach to mobile power — designed to generate electricity when needed while reducing energy-system weight and dependence on conventional charging logistics.

Our work builds on decades of experience in electrochemistry, energy conversion, thermodynamics, and advanced power-system engineering.

LEADERSHIP & R&D

Founder & Chief System Architect

ProtonX Power LLC is led by a Ph.D. system architect with 35+ years of R&D experience spanning energy conversion systems, thermodynamics, fuel cells, electrochemical technologies, and advanced power architectures.

His experience spans academic research and global industry, including work associated with the University of Illinois (UIUC) and LG Electronics.
