POWER ARCHITECTURE

Carry the Means. Generate the Energy.

4 CORE PRINCIPLES
FIELD-ACTIVATED ENERGY SYSTEM

Generate
Power When
You Need It.

ProtonX Power LLC is developing a different approach to mission power: instead of carrying all of the energy as stored electrochemical energy, the mission carries the means to generate power when required.

The architecture separates the reusable power-generation module from inactive, dry consumable fuel. Additional mission duration can therefore be supported by additional fuel rather than proportionally increasing the mass of stored battery energy.

The objective is a power system that can be activated at the point of use and scaled to mission-specific energy requirements.

UP TO 90%LOWER CARRIED SYSTEM WEIGHT*

ProtonX Power LLC is targeting a substantial reduction in the carried mass associated with extended-duration power requirements compared with comparable conventional battery-based configurations.

The design objective is to shift mission energy logistics from carrying increasing amounts of stored battery energy toward carrying a reusable generation module and lightweight, inactive consumable fuel.

*Conceptual development target relative to comparable conventional battery-based configurations. Actual system weight, energy output, and performance are subject to prototype development and validation testing.

MISSION CONCEPT

Power Longer. Carry Less.

4 STAGES

The ProtonX system concept is built around a simple change in mission energy logistics: carry the reusable power-generation module and inactive fuel, then activate and generate electricity when power is required.

01 / CARRY

Carry

Reusable Module + Dry Solid Fuel Pouch

The mission carries a reusable power-generation module together with lightweight, inactive dry consumable fuel.

02 / ACTIVATE

Activate

At the Point of Use

Field activation using locally available aqueous fluids, where appropriate. The activation approach is intended to reduce dependence on dedicated pre-positioned activation resources.

03 / GENERATE

Generate

Power On Demand

The activated system generates electricity for the connected mission load. Additional endurance can be supported by additional inactive consumable fuel.

04 / RETURN

Return

Minimize Reverse Logistics

Spent consumable material is designed to avoid the return logistics associated with discharged battery banks, subject to applicable handling and environmental requirements.

BATTERY-BASED MISSION

More Mission Time
Means More Stored Energy

CARRY→ CONSUME→ REPLACE→ RECHARGE

Extending a mission can require additional battery capacity to be carried from the beginning, increasing the amount of energy-storage mass and creating additional charging and resupply requirements.

PROTONX SYSTEM MISSION CONCEPT

More Mission Time
Can Mean More Fuel

CARRY→ ACTIVATE→ GENERATE→ REPEAT

Carry the reusable generation module once, then add inactive dry solid fuel pouch as mission duration requires. Spent consumable material is designed to avoid the return logistics associated with discharged battery banks, subject to applicable handling and environmental requirements.
