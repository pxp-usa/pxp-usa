Technology & Core Capabilities

Four engineering principles define every ProtonX Power system, from cartridge chemistry to stack architecture.

CAP-01

Ultra-High Energy Density

A dramatically better weight-to-power ratio than lithium-ion, cutting the load and bulk carried by tactical personnel and unmanned platforms alike.

CAP-02

Instant On-Demand Activation

Zero self-discharge across long-term storage. Full power output is available in the field with a single activation step, whenever it's needed.

CAP-03

Rugged & Safe Solid-Chemical Energy

Built on stable aqueous and solid-chemical storage rather than high-pressure hydrogen gas, eliminating explosion and fire risk while holding up in harsh conditions.

CAP-04

Proprietary High-Efficiency Architecture

A patent-pending cell and stack design minimizes fuel loss and drives energy conversion efficiency well beyond conventional fuel cell layouts.
